كيف تختار استوديو تصميم مناسب لمشروعك؟

AY. Design أيمن الأستاذ إرشادات

Criterion One: A Real Portfolio

Depth matters more than volume. Are there Case Studies explaining the thinking? A serious designer takes pride in their process.

Criterion Two: Process, Not Just Output

A serious studio explains its methodology clearly. Chaos in process produces chaos in output.

Criterion Three: Questions Before Design

A professional asks dozens of questions before drawing a line. An amateur opens Photoshop in the first meeting.

Criterion Four: Revision Policy

  • No open promises: Unlimited revisions signals lack of respect for time.
  • No over-restriction: No revisions after first approval unnecessarily frightens clients.
  • Two or three clear rounds with defined scope.

Criterion Five: Human Chemistry

If the first meeting feels off, it won't get better — it will worsen.

Choosing a designer is not like buying a product — it is like hiring a temporary team member. Choose with both your mind and your instinct.

Questions to Ask Before Signing

  • Can I see a complete case study for a similar project?
  • What is your step-by-step work process?
  • What file formats will I receive at delivery?
  • Does the service include brand usage guidelines?
