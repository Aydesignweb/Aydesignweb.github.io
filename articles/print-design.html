Material Selection

  • Gloss-coated: Reflects colors intensely — suited for restaurants and consumer products.
  • Matte-coated: Luxury, refined feel — ideal for premium brands.
  • Kraft: Naturalness and authenticity.
  • Thick board: Weight and stature for premium business cards.

Special Techniques

  • Foil Stamping: Gold or silver layer that draws the eye instantly.
  • Embossing: A raised impression felt by touch.
  • Laser Cutting: Turns print into artwork.
  • Spot UV: Gloss on specific areas — striking contrast.
