3. Identity Consistency — Building Memory

Every post must be a natural extension of brand identity. Consistency builds recognition.

5. Platform Data — Designing from Reality

Common Social Media Design Mistakes

  • Relying on templates: Erases your distinction.
  • Too much text: Social media is visual first.
  • Wrong dimensions: Each platform has its requirements.
  • Constant style changes: Erases accumulated visual memory.
